## How to sign

Email **legal@univec.ai** from the address on your GitHub account, with
subject `postvec CLA`, stating which form you sign (Individual or Entity),
your full name, GitHub username, and (for the Entity form) the legal name of
the organization. Keep a copy. Univec will confirm in writing. Open a pull
request against `postvec-server/` only after that confirmation.

Do not submit a pull request against `postvec-server/` as a way of signing.
